Microbial Genomics & Bioinformatics

The research group behind PAAMPdb, working at the intersection of microbial genomics, bioinformatics, antimicrobial resistance, reverse vaccinology, and machine learning.

Inregrative Biology Lab

We are based at Atta-ur-Rahman School of Applied Biosciences (ASAB), National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), a lush green campus in the beautiful Capital City Islamabad. Our group is mainly focusing on integrative genomics approaches; producing and analyzing the public genomic data for studying the genomic epidemiology, evolution and transmission in bacterial pathogens.
We are particularly interested in the regional crisis of growing antimicrobial resistance (AMR) and dissemination of multidrug resistant pathogens both at the community and healthcare settings. We are also like to develop tools and databases for pathogen genomic data analysis, prospective surveillance and tracking of emerging pathogens in the environment and clinical settings using whole genome sequencing and bioinformatics analysis. Employing reverse vaccinology and machine learning approaches for prediction of potential immunogenic and therapeutic targets (vaccines and drugs). For more information about our lab and current research projects, please visit our website.
